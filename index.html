Bollanda

Bollanda is an international knowledge brand and starting point for flower bulbs, helping gardeners choose the right bulbs and learn how to plant, care for and store them with clear, species-specific guides.

  • Subject: flower bulbs (bulbs, corms, tubers and rhizomes)
  • Publisher: Maestroma, with Bollanda as its registered trade name
  • Founder: Joachim van Rossenberg
